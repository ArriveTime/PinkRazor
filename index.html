"We Stop Asking For Permission": Elena Vance on Quantum Security & Scaling

PUBLISHED SEPT 4, 2026 • BY MAYA CHEN

PinkRazor: Elena, you raised a $45M Series B in a market where hard-tech investments were historically down. What was the critical turning point in pitch rooms?

Elena Vance: "We stopped apologizing for being early or technical. Instead of leading with market sizing slides, we brought our live threat-detection hardware onto the table and executed a real-time breach simulation. Proof of execution beats pitch decks every time."

PinkRazor: What advice do you give female founders facing patronizing line of questioning from syndicate partners?

Elena Vance: "Reframe the conversation immediately. If they ask about downside risks while asking male founders about market upside, flip the script: 'While our risk-mitigation framework is best-in-class, let me walk you through how we capture 40% market share over 36 months.' Own the growth narrative."
